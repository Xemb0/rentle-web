Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Imautotech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Rentle application ("the App"). Rentle is a property and rent management app for owners and tenants. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Account Sign-In Data

You create an account using email, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple (via Supabase auth). We receive your name, email address, and a unique user ID to identify your account.

For Google we request only the minimum scopes for sign-in (openid, profile, email). You can revoke access at any time.

Property & Tenancy Data

Owners add properties, rooms, tenants, bills, and payment records. Tenants are associated with a room via an invite code. This data is stored in our Supabase backend so both parties can access what's relevant to them.

Documents (KYC & uploads)

Tenants may upload identity/KYC and other documents, which are stored securely in Supabase Storage and shared only with the associated property owner.

Payments

Rent and bill payments are made over UPI directly between the tenant and the owner's bank/UPI account. Payment is processed by your UPI app and bank — we do not collect or store your card or bank credentials. We record payment metadata (amount, date, status) to generate receipts and reports. Optional premium subscriptions are sold as in-app purchases and managed via RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous user ID.

Device, Usage & Diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand usage and diagnose problems, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications. This may include device model, OS version, app version, crash reports, and feature-usage events.

Advertising

Free use of Rentle is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and device information to deliver ads. On iOS, ads are only personalised with your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Information obtained through account sign-in is never used to personalise ads, and a premium subscription removes ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Limited Use of Google User Data

Rentle's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, allow humans to read it (except with consent, for security, or where anonymised), or use it to train generalised AI models.

Permissions We Require

Camera

To scan an owner's invite QR code and to capture KYC/document photos. Active only when you use those features.

Photos & Files

To let you select and upload documents (KYC, receipts) from your device.

Notifications

To alert you about new bills, payments, messages, and reminders.

Internet

Required for sign-in, syncing your data, payments, subscriptions, and notifications.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data from inside the App: Settings → Delete Account. You may also email us at support@imautotech.in to request deletion.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, realtime
  • Firebase — analytics, crash reporting, push notifications
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • RevenueCat — subscription management

Data Retention & Security

We retain your data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, and delete it on request. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes appear on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
