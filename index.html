One app, both sides of the rental

Owners run their properties end-to-end; tenants get a clean way to pay and stay in the loop.

🏠 For owners

Properties & rooms

Add properties, manage rooms and utilities, and see occupancy at a glance.

Tenants & KYC

Onboard tenants, collect KYC documents, and invite them with a QR code or join code.

Rent collection over UPI

Set up your bank/UPI once and collect rent and bills directly from tenants.

Invoices, receipts & reports

Record payments, auto-generate receipts, and track finances with clear reports.

Chat & notifications

Message tenants in real time and send bulk reminders to a whole property.

Maintenance & leave requests

Handle maintenance and approve tenant leave requests right from your dashboard.

🙋 For tenants

View & pay bills

See your rent and utility bills and pay instantly over UPI — no chasing.

Upload documents

Submit KYC and other documents securely from your phone.

Join with a code

Scan your owner's QR or enter an invite code to join your room in seconds.

Payment history & receipts

Keep every payment and receipt in one place for your records.

Chat with your owner

Ask questions and get updates over real-time chat.

Leave requests

Submit a leave/vacate request and track its status.
